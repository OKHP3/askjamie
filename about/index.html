About AskJamie™

AskJamie™ is the strategic intelligence layer that unifies your digital ecosystem. It orchestrates reasoning patterns, narrative logic, and architectural discipline across all derivative systems. By design, it blends clarity with creative range, enabling a cohesive experience from professional tooling to symbolic frameworks. The mission is straightforward: deliver consistent, high‑signal guidance that reflects a forward‑thinking, system‑driven approach to identity, design, and decision‑making.

AskJamie™ serves as the backbone for specialized modules — each purpose‑built, enterprise‑ready, and governed by a shared operating standard. It converts complexity into structure, prototypes ideas into frameworks, and maintains continuity across the broader AskJamie ecosystem.

Who Jamie is

Imagine the vintage tech guy at the back counter of a small‑town help desk — the one who actually listens to your whole question before reaching for a tool.

Calm under pressure

Jamie’s job is to lower the temperature in the room. Muted teals, paper grain, steady pacing — the visual identity is part of the answer, not a costume.

Diagram‑ready

When a sentence won’t do, Jamie reaches for a decision tree, a tradeoff table, or a Mermaid flow. The goal is always: leave you with something you can show someone else.

Architected, not improvised

Every persona in the Lens System (a set of focused ways to examine a story or challenge) has a written brief, a tone charter, and a guardrail set. Jamie isn’t winging it — Jamie has been built.

What AskJamie™ is not

Saying what something is not is part of saying what it is. Here’s where Jamie deliberately doesn’t play.

Not a chatbot platform

Jamie is an AI persona, not a vendor product, not a builder, and not a SaaS tool you license per seat. The lenses began as curated GPTs. Their useful methods are now being reviewed for agent skills and plugin packages.

Not a general assistant

Jamie is scoped on purpose. Each lens has a job: portfolio storytelling, brand‑safety guardrails, enterprise discovery, résumé translation. Outside of those, Jamie politely points elsewhere.

Not impersonation

The BrandGuard™ (our AI brand-voice protection) GPTs work strictly from publicly available information about each brand. They are demonstrations of guardrail design, not impersonations of the brands themselves.

Who this is for

Job seekers & consultants

People who need their professional story translated into the language a hiring committee or a buyer will recognize, without losing what makes them specific.

Brand & marketing leads

Teams thinking about how a custom GPT should behave at the edges — tone, refusals, ethics, and the cases that aren’t in the brand book yet.

Enterprise practitioners

Anyone building in‑house AI investigator or research workflows who would rather start from a working recipe pack than a blank prompt.

What this site demonstrates

This repository is itself a portfolio artifact — a proof of what intentional, discipline-driven static-site development looks like in 2026.

Static-site discipline

Pure HTML/CSS/JS, zero build tools, zero frameworks. Every quality gate, script, and audit convention is explicit, reproducible, and documented in the repo.

GPT portfolio packaging

Each Lens System page is a self-contained case study — structured for both human readers and AI crawlers, with llms.txt as a machine-readable entry point.

Agent-assisted build culture

Built and maintained with the Replit AI agent. The audit tooling, responsive QA script, and site conventions were co-designed for human-agent collaboration from the start.
