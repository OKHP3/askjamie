· GPT‑AJ01: Résumé Representative

Give your experience a voice.

Your résumé opens the conversation. Imagine a representative that can help tell the story behind it.

AskJamie™ Résumé Representative began as a career-focused custom GPT prototype. Its bigger idea: make your experience easier to explain, adapt, and discuss.

More than a list of jobs.

A career contains decisions, lessons, and connections that do not always fit on a page. The Résumé Representative explores how a conversational assistant can help bring that context forward.

Start with evidence

Bring together the experience, achievements, and examples you can substantiate. Your story stays yours to review and shape.

Adapt to the opportunity

Explore how your experience relates to a role, then draft a tailored résumé or cover letter for your own review.

Practice the conversation

Use questions and feedback to prepare for an interview, clarify examples, and find a more natural way to explain your work.

What is a custom GPT?

Think of ChatGPT as a general-purpose conversation tool. A custom GPT gives it a particular job, instructions, and, when provided, reference files. Those materials guide its answers; they do not retrain the underlying model or make its behavior perfectly predictable.

A role

Instructions describe the task, the intended audience, and the tone and boundaries the assistant should try to follow.

Relevant material

Reference files can provide background for a response. Choose information carefully and check what your host makes accessible.

Your judgment

AI can misunderstand, omit, or invent details. Review every result against your own evidence before relying on it.

The prototype and the bigger blueprint

GPT‑AJ01 began as a prototype around Jamie Hill’s career. The broader product idea is a do-it-yourself blueprint that would help another person build a representative around their own experience. A buyer kit, its final contents, and its supported host have not been announced as available.

Instructions to adapt

A proposed playbook would explain how to set a role, voice, and evidence-first boundaries for your own assistant.

Templates for your story

Adaptable knowledge templates could help organize your career history, work examples, and the requirements of a role.

A repeatable process

Guidance could bring together résumé and cover-letter drafting, interview practice, and review for each new opportunity.

Imagine a representative for each opportunity.

The original vision goes beyond tailoring a document. A candidate could prepare a separate, role-specific conversational representative and invite a hiring manager to ask about the experience most relevant to that job.

Prepare

Start with reviewed career evidence and a job description. Identify the experience and examples that fit the role.

Specialize

Envision a separate representative for a role or career path, with only the information you approve for that audience.

Share thoughtfully

A recruiter might ask, “Which projects best match this role?” A real sharing workflow would need recipient access, privacy, and answer quality tested before use.

Today’s platform boundary

Job-specific GPT copies were part of the original concept. OpenAI says personal ChatGPT accounts cannot create new GPTs, and custom GPTs are scheduled for retirement. Account and workspace rules differ. A tested replacement or recruiter-sharing service has not been announced here. See OpenAI’s sharing guidance and retirement FAQ.

Useful assistance. Human accountability.

A reusable workflow can complement professional advice and give you a starting point for each role. Its value depends on the evidence you provide, the platform you use, and your review.

Keep claims accurate

Verify dates, qualifications, metrics, and every generated statement. Do not submit invented experience or achievements.

Separate what you share

Keep sensitive material out of any recipient-facing version. Check the host’s data, access, and sharing rules first. A prompt is not an access control.

Know the limits

AI cannot guarantee privacy, an interview, an ATS result, or a job offer. Platform access, availability, and costs may change.
